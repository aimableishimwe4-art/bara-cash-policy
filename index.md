## 1. Information we process

- Mobile money transaction information, including amounts, fees, balances, dates, transaction references, and sender or recipient details.
- Budget information, categories, labels, and other records you create.

- Contact names and phone numbers when you use contact selection features.
- Account information associated with sign-in, such as your name, email address, and account identifier.
- Contribution group information, including membership, pledges, payment records, and progress.

## 2. SMS access

Bara Cash requests SMS access to identify supported mobile money transaction messages and create financial records automatically.

This supports transaction history, balance estimates, expense tracking, fee calculations, budgets, and reports.

SMS access is intended for supported financial transaction messages. Unrelated personal messages are not used for advertising or profiling.

You can revoke SMS permission through your device settings. Automatic transaction detection will then be limited or unavailable.

## 4. How we use information

We use information to:

- Provide transaction tracking, budgets, and financial reports.
- Display supported account balances and spending summaries.
- Support recipient selection and user-initiated payment actions.
- Provide sign-in and contribution group features.
- Maintain the app and respond to support requests.

Balance estimates depend on available transaction messages and may differ from your provider’s current balance.

## 5. Payments

Payments initiated through Bara Cash are processed by your mobile money operator through its own services, including USSD where supported.

Bara Cash does not hold your mobile money funds. Your operator’s terms and privacy policy apply to its payment services.

Do not send your mobile money PIN or account passwords to our support team.
