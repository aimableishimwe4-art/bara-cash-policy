# Privacy Policy for Bara Cash

Effective date: 12 November 2026

Bara Cash is an independently operated personal finance app for mobile money users in Rwanda.

This policy explains what information Bara Cash processes, why it is needed, how it is shared, and how you can request deletion.

For privacy, support or deletion requests, contact **info.baracash@gmail.com**.

## 1. Information we process

Depending on the features you use, Bara Cash processes:

- Mobile money transaction amounts, fees, dates, balances, references, and sender or recipient details.
- Budgets, categories, labels, descriptions, favourites and other records you create.
- SIM subscription information needed to distinguish supported accounts.
- Contact names and phone numbers when you use contact selection.
- Google account information used for sign-in, including your name, email address and account identifier.
- Shared contribution information, including group membership, pledges, payments and payment status.

## 2. SMS access and automatic tracking

With your permission, Bara Cash reads supported mobile money transaction SMS to create financial records, calculate fees, estimate balances and update reports.

For shared contributions, relevant extracted payment details can be submitted for matching and recording contributions.

Full SMS messages, unrelated personal messages, personal budgets, unrelated transactions and wallet balances are not uploaded through the shared-contribution matching process.

You can decline SMS access, pause automatic tracking, or revoke permission in Android settings. This stops the relevant automatic tracking functions but does not erase records already saved.

Bara Cash does not use your SMS or financial information for advertising.

## 3. Local storage

Personal transaction records, budgets, allocations and local funds are stored on your device.

Recorded transactions are separate from the original SMS messages. Deleting an original SMS is not a request to delete its previously recorded transaction.

Local information remains until deleted or the app’s stored data is removed. Keep exported reports somewhere secure. Exports may not contain everything needed to restore your app.

## 4. Sign-in and shared contributions

Bara Cash uses Google sign-in and Supabase to support accounts and shared contribution features.

Google sign-in verifies your Google identity. It does not verify ownership of a mobile money number.

Shared services process account identifiers, group details, membership, pledges, contribution records and relevant payment evidence. Payment evidence can include the transaction reference, amount, payment time and account numbers.

Group members can see participant names, pledges, payments, transaction references, payment status and relevant correction or activity information. The organiser’s receiving number is displayed for payments.

Avoid putting sensitive personal information in group names, descriptions or correction reasons.

## 5. Payments

Bara Cash can initiate payment actions through your mobile money operator’s services, including USSD where supported.

Bara Cash does not hold your mobile money funds or control withdrawals. Your operator processes payments and handles payment authorisation.

Never send your mobile money PIN, Google password or verification codes to Bara Cash support.

Balances and contribution confirmations depend on available records and are not an independent guarantee of provider settlement.

## 6. How information is used and disclosed

Information is used to provide transaction tracking, budgets, reports, account access and shared contributions.

Information may be disclosed:

- To Google and Supabase as needed for the features they support.
- To contribution group participants as described above.
- To recipients or applications you choose when sharing or exporting information.
- Where required by applicable law.

We do not sell users’ personal information or use it for advertising.

Online service providers may process information outside Rwanda.

## 7. Retention and deletion

Personal records are retained on your device until deleted or the app’s stored data is removed.

Online account and shared contribution records are retained to support the account and group history until deletion is requested or the records are otherwise removed. Closing a contribution fund does not itself erase its history.

To request account and associated online data deletion, email **info.baracash@gmail.com**, preferably from the email address used to sign in. Use the subject **Bara Cash account deletion**.

We may request information necessary to verify ownership. Never provide a password or mobile money PIN.

Deletion requests are handled manually. We aim to complete verified requests within 30 days.

Deleting an organiser’s account can also remove groups they own and associated records, affecting other members. We will explain this consequence when handling the request.

Deleting online data does not automatically erase copies stored on your device, exported reports or copies held by other people. Uninstalling Bara Cash does not request online account deletion.

## 8. Security

Bara Cash uses protected sign-in token storage on supported Android devices and authenticated access controls for shared services.

No storage or transmission system can guarantee absolute security. Protect your phone, Google account and exported reports against unauthorised access.

## 9. Your choices and rights

You can manage Android permissions, pause SMS tracking and choose whether to use shared features or export information.

Contact **info.baracash@gmail.com** to request access, correction, deletion or restriction of online personal information, or to raise a privacy concern.

You may also raise concerns with Rwanda’s Data Protection and Privacy Office.

## 10. Age requirement

Bara Cash is intended for users aged 18 and over.

If you believe someone under 18 has provided personal information through Bara Cash, contact **info.baracash@gmail.com** so we can investigate and take appropriate action.
